About Accident AI

Accident AI is an accident risk prediction system designed to identify possible accident-prone situations. The system uses live location, weather, traffic, road condition, vehicle speed and time to estimate accident risk.


When the risk is high, the system displays an emergency alert with the current location. The user can also open the location in Google Maps and use the family call option.
